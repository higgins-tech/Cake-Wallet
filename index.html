Frequently Asked Questions

Cake Wallet is a free, open-source, non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet available on iOS, Android, Linux, and Windows. It supports Monero (XMR), Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Litecoin (LTC), and many other coins, giving you full control of your private keys and digital assets.

Cake Wallet supports a wide range of cryptocurrencies including XMR, BTC, ETH, LTC, USDT, Haven (XHV), NANO, and more. Check our full list of supported assets in the app or on our documentation page.

Download Cake Wallet from your preferred platform, create a new wallet or restore an existing one using your seed phrase, and start sending and receiving crypto immediately. Our in-app guides and documentation will walk you through every step.

You can reach our support team at support@cakewallet.com. You can also join our community on Telegram or Reddit where both our team and community members are happy to help with any questions you have.
